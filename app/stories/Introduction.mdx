<p className="intro-eyebrow">Design System</p>
<h1 className="intro-title">Honest</h1>

<p className="intro-lead">
  A design system built for a portfolio site that needed to feel like me — not like every other designer portfolio out there.
</p>

<p className="intro-body">
  I needed something accessible and modern that moved away from the clichéd styles I kept seeing across the industry. Most portfolio sites look the same: dark mode, heavy gradients, oversized type, and visual tricks designed to impress rather than communicate. I wanted something different — professional, mature, and grounded in real design system thinking.
</p>

<p className="intro-body">
  Honest is the result. A warm, editorial system built on semantic tokens, a rem-based type scale, and components that map 1:1 between Figma and code. Every decision is documented, every token is intentional, and nothing is there just to look impressive.
</p>

<p className="intro-body">

</p>
